Rada Privacy Policy

Effective 24 September 2026

Rada ("the app") is a driving-awareness app for Kenyan drivers, developed and operated by Derrick Wadek. This policy explains what information Rada collects, how it's used, and the choices available to you. Rada is a personal, independently-developed app — not part of a larger company or ad network.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account, Rada collects a username (or, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and email address those services share with us) and a profile photo if one is available. Rada uses Firebase Authentication to manage sign-in; a plain username is converted to an internal address that never receives mail, so Rada does not require a real email address unless you choose Google or Apple sign-in.

Location data. While you're on an active drive, Rada reads your device's precise (GPS) location to compare it against known speed-camera locations and nearby community reports, and to power the map and drive statistics shown in the app. Location is read only while Rada is actively tracking a drive, through a foreground service with a visible ongoing notification — never silently in the background.

Community reports. If you report a camera, checkpoint or alcohol-check, Rada stores the report's type, coordinates and timestamp, linked to your account.

Drive and usage statistics. Rada keeps a monthly count of warnings you received, alerts you avoided, and an estimated fines-avoided figure, tied to your account.

Subscription status. If you subscribe to Rada Pro, Google Play shares a purchase token and subscription status with Rada's backend so it can verify your entitlement. Rada does not see or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Google Play.

Diagnostic data. Rada uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and basic device/OS information automatically when the app crashes, to help fix bugs.

How location data is used

Location is Rada's core feature: it's how the app knows when you're near a speed camera or a recently reported checkpoint or alcohol-check, so it can warn you in time. Location is:

  • Read only while a drive is actively being tracked, through a foreground service with a persistent notification — you always know when it's running, and you can stop it at any time.
  • Never collected in the background when you're not driving. Rada does not request Android's "Allow all the time" background location permission.
  • Not sold, and not shared with advertisers. It's used only to power in-app alerts, your drive statistics, and (if you choose to report something) the coordinates attached to that report.
  • Not retained as a continuous location history. Rada does not store a trail of everywhere you've driven — only the aggregate monthly counts described above, and, if you submit one, the location of that specific report.

Third-party services

Rada is built on a small number of third-party services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

Firebase Authentication (Google)Sign-in (username/password, Google, or Apple)
Cloud Firestore (Google)Stores account, report and drive-statistics data
Cloud Functions (Google)Verifies Google Play purchases and updates subscription status

Google Play BillingProcesses Rada Pro subscription payments

Google's privacy policy for these services is at policies.google.com/privacy. Rada does not integrate any advertising SDK and does not use any analytics service beyond Crashlytics.

Data sharing and disclosure

Rada does not sell your personal information, and does not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes. Information is shared only:

  • With the service providers above (Firebase, Google Play Billing), strictly to operate the app's core features.
  • With other Rada users, in an anonymous, aggregated form: a report you submit is shown to nearby drivers as a pin on the map with its type, location and time — never with your name, username or account identifier attached.
  • If required by law, to comply with a valid legal request.

Data retention and deletion

Account data, reports and drive statistics are kept for as long as your account exists, so Rada can keep working the way you'd expect (your trial or subscription status, your history of avoided fines, and so on). If you'd like your account and its data deleted, see how to request account deletion — it's completed within 30 days. Crash reports collected by Crashlytics are retained under Google's own Crashlytics retention policy.

Security

Rada's data is protected by Firestore security rules that restrict every account to its own data: no one can read another account's reports, statistics or trial status, and no client — including the app itself — can grant itself Pro access or edit its own trial. Purchase records are written only by Rada's server-side Cloud Functions, never directly by the app. Rada also does not request Android's auto-backup permission, so account data isn't copied off your device through a system backup.

Your choices and rights

  • Location permission can be denied or revoked at any time in Android's app settings; Rada simply won't be able to show live alerts without it.
  • Deleting a report you submitted is available directly from the app, and removes it immediately.
  • Access, correction or deletion of your account data can be requested by emailing derrickwadek@gmail.com.
  • Managing your subscription (cancelling, viewing renewal dates, requesting a refund) is done through Google Play's own subscription settings, since all payments are processed by Google Play Billing.

Children's privacy

Rada is intended for licensed drivers and is not directed at children. Rada does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided Rada with personal information, contact derrickwadek@gmail.com and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Rada's features change. Material changes will be reflected by a new date at the top of this page; continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to derrickwadek@gmail.com.
