Delete your Rada account

How to request deletion of your account and its data

You can ask to have your Rada account and the data tied to it permanently deleted at any time. This is a manual request handled by the developer — Rada doesn't yet have a self-serve delete button in the app.

How to request deletion

Send an email to derrickwadek@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Rada account, from the account you'd like removed if you signed in with Google or Apple, or including your Rada username if you signed in with a username and password.

Email to request deletion

What gets deleted

  • Your account itself (sign-in record) and profile information (username or Google/Apple name, photo).
  • Your trial and subscription status stored in Rada's database.
  • Your monthly drive statistics (warnings received, alerts avoided, estimated fines avoided).
  • The camera, checkpoint and alcohol-check reports you submitted.

Deletion is completed within 30 days of the request.

What isn't deleted by this request

  • If you subscribed to Rada Pro, Google Play retains its own records of the purchase (for receipts, refunds and tax purposes) under Google's policies — those aren't controlled by Rada and aren't affected by an account-deletion request. You can manage or cancel a subscription any time from Google Play's own subscription settings.
  • Crash reports already sent to Firebase Crashlytics before your request may persist for a short period under Google's own Crashlytics retention policy; they aren't linked to your name or account.

See the full Privacy Policy for more detail on what Rada collects and how it's used.
